Welcome to DTDB!

This database is the data collection platform and visualization platform of this article (Drug-driven reclassification of multiple tumour subtypes reveals intrinsic molecular concordance of therapy across histologically disparate cancers). This study demonstrates how pharmacogenomic data can be used to systematically classify cancers in terms of response to various compounds. The users can view tumour classifications with a purely therapy-oriented perspective independent of histology subtypes, as well as the intrinsic concordance of molecular mechanisms. Start using DTDB by searching for a drug, cell line, or cancer type.

e.g. Dabrafenib

DTDB is developed by the Haiyun Wang's Lab at the School of Life Sciences and Technology, Tongji University, Shanghai, China.
